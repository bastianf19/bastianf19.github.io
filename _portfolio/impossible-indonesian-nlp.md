### Overview
* **Institution:** University of Edinburgh
* **Timeline:** June – August 2025
* **Advisor / Focus:** Natural Language Processing & Language Acquisition
* **Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, BabyLM Benchmark, GPT-2

### Research Highlights
* Explored whether autoregressive neural language models possess an inductive bias towards natural human syntax or whether they equally memorize linguistically "impossible" grammatical structures.
* Translated the developmental BabyLM English dataset into natural Indonesian to build a developmentally realistic low-resource benchmark.
* Applied systematic linguistic perturbations (word-order shuffling and reverse syntax perturbation) to generate synthetically impossible Indonesian grammars.
* Trained GPT-2 models across natural vs. perturbed conditions to analyze learning curves, perplexity dynamics, and grammatical generalization limits.
