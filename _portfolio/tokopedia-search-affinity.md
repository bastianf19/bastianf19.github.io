### Overview
* **Role:** Data Scientist - Search Algorithm
* **Company:** ByteDance Global E-commerce / Tokopedia (Jakarta, Indonesia)
* **Timeline:** Nov 2021 – Aug 2024
* **Tech Stack:** Python, SQL, GCP, Jenkins, Airflow, Hadoop, Milvus, HQL, Machine Learning Classifiers

### Key Contributions & Achievements
* **Product-to-City Affinity Model:**
  * Built end-to-end classification pipelines connecting geographic buyer affinity to merchant distribution.
  * Orchestrated training and batch inference pipelines on Google Cloud Platform, Jenkins, and Apache Airflow.
  * Drove a **+12% increase in sales conversions** and a **+8% increase in customer impressions** for regional merchants.
* **Preferred Merchant Model:**
  * Modeled and simulated seller growth trajectories to prioritize high-trust merchants, resulting in an internal test revenue uplift of **+2.36%**.
* **Instant & Same-Day Fresh Product Discovery:**
  * Re-ranked logistics-sensitive product inventories to optimize instant and same-day delivery conversion.
  * Boosted conversions by **+11% for short-distance**, **+4% for medium-distance**, and **+0.8% overall**.
* **ByteDance Infrastructure Integration:**
  * Migrated and integrated Tokopedia core search features into global ByteDance infrastructure using Hadoop, Milvus vector database, and HQL.
