### Overview
* **Institution:** University of Edinburgh
* **Timeline:** January – March 2025
* **Tech Stack:** Python, BART, BERTweet, PyTorch, Transformers, Social Media NLP

### Key Contributions & Architecture
* Conducted extensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on social media text streams from X to detect structural, sentiment, and lexical markers associated with misleading information.
* Implemented and benchmarked:
  * **Single-Tower Architecture:** Fine-tuned domain-specific transformers (BERTweet) for direct text classification.
  * **Two-Tower Architecture:** Evaluated dual-encoder setups using BART representations to measure claim-to-context semantic similarity and debunking relevance.
* Demonstrated high precision in early detection windows before viral social spread.
