## Abstract

Classifying short texts such as scientific article titles presents a significant challenge due to sparsity, lack of context, and high domain specificity. In this research, we explore the integration of graph models with deep learning architectures to extract representative topic labels and classify short texts effectively.

Using a combination of SQL and Python for data collection and preprocessing, we experimented with graph-based deep learning methods including **DeepLDA** and **Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN)** on researcher and publication datasets. The proposed method improves representation richness and classification accuracy for title-based topic labeling.
